# Selection and portfolio feasibility frontier

S2b-a.1 asks what would have to be true for hypothetical selection and large
portfolio construction to lower a principal-floor claim enough for external
risk transfer, while separate commercial assumptions permit PlatformCo profit.

The model order is:

```text
frozen S2b-a empirical terminal marginal
    -> hypothetical selection
    -> equal-weight portfolio construction
    -> macro and sector factor dependence
    -> terminal NAV
    -> principal-floor claim
    -> separate PlatformCo scenario economics
```

The central comparison is 1,000 broad empirical venture companies, 1,000
hyper-selected AI companies, and 1,000 hyper-selected frontier-technology
companies. None of the selection, dependence, price, demand, founder acceptance,
fee, cost or valuation inputs is represented as an observed fact.

This milestone is a feasibility frontier, not insurance pricing, a solvency
model, a legal structure, a forecast or a valuation.
