# Limits and falsification

The model actively searches for failure conditions:

- dependence can erase the benefit of large N;
- AI concentration can cost more than selection saves;
- selection can disappear or revert to the population marginal;
- removing mega-winners can expose reliance on power-law capture;
- macro and sector shocks can dominate company-level screening;
- a liquidity freeze is distinct from the terminal claim;
- founder dilution and low acceptance can reduce economic contribution;
- external pricing can eliminate arrangement margin;
- demand may remain 1x;
- fixed and variable costs can make every tested issuance scale unprofitable.

Unknowns include actual underwriting skill, unique-company dependence,
investment timing, censoring, liquidity, founder willingness, demand response,
risk-carrier terms, legal enforceability, capital treatment and operating cost.
The model reports the values these unknowns would need to take. It does not
assume they are attainable.
