# Decision 0001: keep the economic core wrapper-neutral

The model uses abstract founder-warrant, reserve and generic stop-loss terms. It does not assume Luxembourg, UK, SPV, insurance, structured-note or reinsurance implementation. Legal and commercial hypotheses are represented as provenance-labelled assumptions and remain outside the mathematical identities.

Reason: the research question concerns economic feasibility first, while legal enforceability, capital treatment and counterparty obligations require separate evidence.
